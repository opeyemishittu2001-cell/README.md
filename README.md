Tips for Promoting Templates
Create a strong preview image and live demo.
Clearly explain what is included in the template.
Show different pages and responsive layouts.
Target a specific audience instead of everyone.
Share useful content around the problem your template solves.
Build a portfolio and personal brand alongside your products.
Participate in relevant communities instead of posting only promotional links.
Test different titles, thumbnails, and descriptions.
Promote your templates consistently rather than only once.
